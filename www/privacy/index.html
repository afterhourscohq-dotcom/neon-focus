Privacy Policy

Neon Focus, by After Hours Co LLC · Effective November 14, 2026

The short version: Neon Focus does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your scores and settings stay on your own device.

What the app stores

To remember your progress, Neon Focus saves a small amount of game data on your device only:

  • Scores, best score, level, XP, streak and daily quest progress
  • Your last 30 Classic runs (score and accuracy) for the Stats screen
  • Your settings (sound, music, vibration, colorblind mode, reduce motion, theme)
  • Whether you have unlocked Pro

This data never leaves your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, erases it permanently.

What we do not do

  • No sign-up, login or account of any kind
  • No advertising, and no advertising IDs
  • No analytics, crash reporting or third-party tracking tools in the app
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone

Purchases

If you buy Neon Focus Pro, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We never receive your payment details. The app only learns whether Pro is unlocked.

The web version

If you play at neonfocus.afterhoursco.dev in a browser, the game works the same way and keeps your data in your browser's local storage. The site is hosted by Vercel, which, like any web host, may process basic technical information such as your IP address to deliver the page and protect against abuse. We do not use that information to identify or track you.

Children

Neon Focus is designed for teens and adults and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If we ever start collecting any data, we will say so here before that version of the app is released.
