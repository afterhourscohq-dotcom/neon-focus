How do I play?
  • Drag anywhere to slide your orb left and right.
  • Catch only what the rule at the top says: a color or a shape.
  • When the rule pill pulses, the rule is about to flip.
  • On HANDS OFF, dodge everything. Holding back earns a bonus.
  • Good catches add time. The game speeds up as you improve and eases off when you struggle.

Tap How to play → Practice round on the home screen any time for a guided run.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Scores, streaks, settings and Pro status are stored locally, so there's no account to make. Deleting the app or clearing its data resets everything, and progress doesn't move between devices.

What does Pro include?

Pro is a one-time unlock, not a subscription: Zen mode, the Daily Challenge, the Stats screen, themes, and every future mini-game. Players who joined during early access keep Pro free forever as founding players.

I bought Pro and it's not showing
  1. Make sure you're signed in to the same Google Play or Apple account you bought it with.
  2. Open Neon Focus Pro from the home screen and tap Restore purchase.
  3. Still missing? Email us with the order number from your purchase receipt.
Refunds

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store, so refunds go through them: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, or reportaproblem.apple.com for Apple. If you have trouble, email us and we'll help.

Sound, motion and colorblind options

Open Settings on the home screen to turn off sound, music or vibration, switch on colorblind mode (blue, orange and white, with marks inside each shape), or reduce motion (no screen shake or flashes).

Is this a treatment for ADHD?

No. Neon Focus is a game designed to be fun for ADHD brains. It is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition.
